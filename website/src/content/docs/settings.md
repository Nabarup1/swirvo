Open Settings from the tray menu (**Settings…**) or the gear in the Board's title bar. Every change applies at once; there's no Save button.

## General

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start with Windows** | Starts Swirvo when you sign in. Off unless you turn it on. |
| **A tip when Swirvo starts** | Shows a reminder of the gesture at start-up. |
| **Theme** | System, Light or Dark. |
| **Install / Uninstall** | Install the copy you're running, or remove it. Store installs say "Installed from the Microsoft Store". |

## Output

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Where new files go** | Next to the original (the default), or a folder you choose. |
| **Quality** | Best, Balanced or Smallest: what a plain drop on a wedge uses. |
| **Show the new file in its folder** | After a wheel conversion, File Explorer opens with the result selected. |

## Wheel

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Which key opens which wheel** | Shift, Ctrl and Alt, in any arrangement. |
| **Pause the wheel** | Stops the wheel from opening until you turn it back on. Also a checkable item in the tray menu. |

## Engines

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Use Microsoft Office / LibreOffice / FFmpeg** | Lets Swirvo use these programs when they're installed, with what it found on this PC. |
| **Files at once** | Auto (one per CPU core) or 1 to 16. |

## Privacy

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Offline mode** | On by default. Nothing in the app uses the network. |
| **Clear the recent-format history** | Forgets which targets you've picked, so the wheel goes back to its starting order. |

## About

The version, the open-source licences of the components Swirvo uses, and a privacy summary. When you [report a problem](https://github.com/Nabarup1/swirvo/issues), the version from here helps.
