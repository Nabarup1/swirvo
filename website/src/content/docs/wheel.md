The wheel is Swirvo's front door. It opens around your pointer while you're dragging, so there's nothing to open first and nowhere to go back to afterwards.

## The gesture

1. **Start dragging** one or more files: from File Explorer, the desktop, an email attachment list, anywhere you can drag files.
2. **Tap a key** while you're still holding the mouse button.
3. **Drop on a wedge.**

| Key | Wheel |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> | Convert: formats |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> | Tools |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> | Actions |

You can choose which key opens which wheel in [Settings](/docs/settings). While a wheel is open, tap another key to switch: the wheel turns a few degrees as it changes, so you notice.

## What a wedge shows

Each wedge has the target in large letters (`JPG`) and a word about what it's good for: *Universal*, *Lossless*, *Web page*, *Plain text*. A wheel holds up to eight wedges. When there are more targets, the last wedge is **More…**, which opens the full list.

The first wedge sits at 12 o'clock and the rest follow clockwise. Swirvo learns which targets you pick and moves them forward, but slowly and with care, so a wedge doesn't jump around and your hand can learn where things are.

## Best, Balanced, Smallest

On the Convert wheel, **rest on a wedge for about half a second**, or push past its outer edge, and a ring of presets opens outside it:

- **Best**: the highest quality the target allows.
- **Balanced**: the default when you just drop on the wedge.
- **Smallest**: the smallest file that still looks right.

Drop on a preset to use it. Your default quality is in [Settings](/docs/settings). Some tools have their own presets: **Compress** for video offers sizes such as 10, 25 and 50 MB.

## Several files

Drag several files at once and the Convert wheel shows the targets they can all reach. Tools and actions work on all of them: **To ZIP** makes one ZIP, **To one PDF** combines images in name order, **Merge** joins PDFs.

## Cancel

Any of these cancels, and nothing is changed:

- press <kbd>Esc</kbd>,
- drop on the middle of the wheel,
- let go outside the wheel.

The wheel fades and the drag carries on as normal, so you can still drop the file on a folder. Swirvo always copies, never moves: dropping on the wheel can't delete or move your original.

## When it's done

Most jobs finish before you've noticed. Swirvo tells you about results in a Windows notification, including the reason when something couldn't be converted. If you'd like File Explorer to show you the new file, turn on **Show the new file in its folder** in [Settings](/docs/settings).

## Pause the wheel

Playing a game or doing a lot of Shift-dragging? Right-click the tray icon and check **Pause the wheel**. It's also in [Settings](/docs/settings).
