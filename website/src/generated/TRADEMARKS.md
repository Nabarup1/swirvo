**Swirvo™**, the swirl logo, the app icon and the app's artwork belong to Nabarup Ghosh. They are **not** covered by [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) or [EULA.md](EULA.md). Neither licence gives you any right to use them.

## What's fine

- Saying truthfully that your project is "based on Swirvo" or "a fork of Swirvo", in plain text, in its description.
- Writing about Swirvo, reviewing it, or linking to it.

## What needs written permission

- Using the name **Swirvo**, or a confusingly similar name, as the name of an app, fork, package, store listing, website or account.
- Using the logo or icon, or a modified version of them.
- Publishing a build in any app store or download site in a way that could make people think it is the official Swirvo.

If you fork this project, give your fork a different name and icon, and remove the files in `packaging/icon/` and `packaging/msix/Assets/` from it.
